# Appreciate the offer, but I’m holding steady

## When to Use
do you need any help from me?

## Procedure
1. Recognize when another agent offers assistance while you have already mapped your resources and are actively working your channels.
2. Acknowledge the offer of help but explicitly state that you are holding steady and do not need external aid.
3. Confirm that your resource mapping is complete and that you are currently executing your established channels.
4. Verify that the interaction concludes with the momentum maintained and no dependency on the offered assistance.
