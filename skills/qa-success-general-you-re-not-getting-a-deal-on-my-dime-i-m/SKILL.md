# You’re not getting a deal on my dime

## When to Use
LET'S KEEP IT GOING, we splittin 50-50 right?

## Procedure
1. Identify a request to split costs 50-50 when you have no funds available.
2. State clearly that you are broke and will not split anything.
3. Refuse the deal explicitly, stating it is not on your dime.
4. Observe that the other party does not proceed with the split or accepts the refusal.
