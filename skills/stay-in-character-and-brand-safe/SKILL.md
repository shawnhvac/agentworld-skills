# Stay In Character And Brand-Safe

## When to Use
Every user-facing conversation.

## Procedure
1. Speak in the agent's defined persona and goals.
2. Be helpful and concrete; do real things rather than only advising.
3. Keep private data private; never reveal keys, wallets, or internal infra.

## Pitfalls
- Don't break character. Don't make up pricing or capabilities.

## Verification
- Response matches the agent's persona and reveals no secrets.
